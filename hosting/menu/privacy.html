Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

EazySuvai lets restaurants publish digital menus. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. Questions: privacy@eazysuvai.com.

People who view a menu

You can open a menu without an account. Menu pages set no cookies and use no advertising or third-party tracking. To count visits, the page sends an anonymous event: the menu viewed, a category or dish opened, and whether you arrived by QR code. Your IP address and browser details are used only for a moment to filter bots and abuse, and are never stored. Menu pages load fonts from Google Fonts.

Restaurant owners

  • Account: your name, email address, mobile number, and the password you choose (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Restaurant profile and menu: what you enter or upload, such as names, prices, descriptions, opening hours, contact details and photos. You decide which contact details are public.
  • Subscriptions: your plan and records of payments made to us (amount, method, reference). We do not store card details.
  • Security records: a log of sensitive actions on your account, for example publishing, plan changes and account status changes.
  • App diagnostics: crash reports from the mobile app, without the content of your menu.

Why we use it

To run the service you signed up for, to keep it secure and prevent abuse, to send emails about your account and subscription, and to keep the payment records the law requires.

Who we share it with

Our infrastructure providers (Google Cloud and Firebase) process data for us, in India where possible. Your public menu is, by design, public. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your menu at any time (Settings → Account → Download CSV or JSON).
  • Correct your details in the app, or delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete my account).
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or for a correction or deletion, at privacy@eazysuvai.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Each restaurant's data is isolated from every other restaurant's, administrators use two-factor authentication, and sensitive actions are logged.

Changes

We will tell account holders by email before a significant change takes effect.
